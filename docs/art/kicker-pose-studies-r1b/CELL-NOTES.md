## What was checked visually

The native contact sheets in both facings were inspected for identity, body volume, joined limbs, hands and the distinction between the two lying poses. The raw walk studies were also checked to resolve near/far leg overlap. This supports presenting candidates; it does not establish game motion or user approval.

Source studies 21 and 22 included low-alpha haze from generation. The documented alpha threshold removes it in the normalized files and sampled cells. The unchanged raw inputs are included for provenance.
