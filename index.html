How to play

Move

← → walk · ↑ ↓ change lane (or WASD)

Attack

J punch · K kick

J+K together: grab and throw. A throw goes through a guard; strikes do not

I Ajsich (kiai): double damage for 4 s when the meter is full. It breaks guards but needs time to recharge

Land hits without getting hit to build a combo
